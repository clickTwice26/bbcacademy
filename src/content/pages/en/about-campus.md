Several important institutions now stand in this part of Kotbari-Mainamati, among them the Bangladesh Academy for Rural Development (BARD), the Mainamati Museum, Cumilla University, an army technology university and a private university.

Our running projects stand on Plot 1 of the Academy's land, and many poor and helpless children benefit from them directly. On Plot 2 we plan a students' hostel, a co-educational English-medium school, a health clinic and an archaeological research centre.
