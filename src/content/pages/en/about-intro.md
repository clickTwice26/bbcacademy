The Bangladesh Buddhist Cultural Academy (BBCA) is a master project of the Young Men's Buddhist Association (YMBA), Cumilla. YMBA was established in 1991 and is registered with the Department of Social Services (Reg. No. Com/481/92) under the Ministry of Social Welfare, Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh. The Academy is always ready to welcome researchers, scholars and tourists from home and abroad.

It is a non-political, non-profit institution for education, religion and culture, socio-economic development and archaeological research. It stands beside the historic Salban Vihara monastery (8th century) at Kotbari, Mainamati, in the district of Cumilla, Bangladesh. The Academy was founded to promote the ancient Buddhist culture and heritage and to propagate Theravada Buddhism in this area.

Kotbari is also a significant tourist centre, located midway between the capital, Dhaka, and the main port city, Chittagong. Visitors can reach us by bus, train or any vehicle from any part of Bangladesh by following Google Maps. Safe and secure dormitory accommodation is available on our premises (charges apply). While here, you can enjoy the natural beauty of Mainamati, the scattered relics of an ancient Buddhist civilisation, and the rare archaeological treasures of the nearby Mainamati Archaeological Museum.
