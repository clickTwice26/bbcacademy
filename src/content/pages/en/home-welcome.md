The Bangladesh Buddhist Cultural Academy (BBCA) is a master project of the Young Men's Buddhist Association (YMBA), Cumilla. It is a non-political, non-profit institution for religion, education, culture, socio-economic development and archaeological research.

The Academy stands beside the historic Salban Vihara monastery (8th century) at Kotbari, Mainamati, in the district of Cumilla. We are always ready to welcome researchers, scholars and tourists from home and abroad.
