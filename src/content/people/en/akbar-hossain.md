Lt Colonel (Retd.) Akbar Hossain, Bir Protik, was born on 18 January 1941 in the district of Cumilla. He began his career at the Military Academy and, after serving in various positions for many years, took voluntary retirement as a Lieutenant Colonel at the end of December 1973. As a politician of the [Bangladesh Nationalist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Nationalist_Party), he served as [Minister for Shipping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Shipping_(Bangladesh)), [Minister for Environment and Forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Environment,_Forest_and_Climate_Change_(Bangladesh)) and [Minister for Petroleum and Mineral Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Power,_Energy_and_Mineral_Resources).

He was a man of secular spirit who shared a deep spiritual bond with the Most Venerable 10th Sangharaj Jyotipal Mahathero. He cherished the lost history, civilisation and culture of the Buddhists of Cumilla, and would often discuss with the great scholar how these could be revived on the world stage.

His inspiration and encouragement led us to establish the Bangladesh Buddhist Cultural Academy. As Minister for Environment and Forests, in response to our earnest appeal, he allocated 2.28 acres of land next to the historic Salban Vihara in Mainamati, Cumilla, for the Academy, and in December 1994 he laid its foundation stone at a holy ceremony and congregation. He remained our beloved guardian and Chief Patron until the moment of his passing. His proud contribution will be written in golden letters in the history of Buddhism in Bangladesh.
