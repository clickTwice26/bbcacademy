Mr. Dhira Sen Singha (Gandhi) was born on 2 April 1947 into a respected Buddhist family in Baraigaon, Laksam, Cumilla, the son of Krishnamohan Singha and Abala Sundari Singha. Raised among the Buddha's teachings, he developed strong moral values, honesty, discipline and compassion from an early age. He earned his Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree with distinction.

After passing the Bangladesh Civil Service (BCS) examination, he began his career with Bangladesh Krishi Bank. He was a sincere and capable banker, but his passion for justice and public service led him down a different path: he resigned from the bank and practised as an advocate at the Cumilla District Judge Court, where he earned wide respect for his integrity, fairness and commitment to justice.

Alongside his legal career he made remarkable contributions to social and religious life. He served as President of the Young Men's Buddhist Association (YMBA) and later as Secretary of the Cumilla Buddhist Association, working tirelessly to promote Buddhist values, preserve cultural heritage, encourage education and strengthen community welfare. His wisdom, humility and selfless service inspired many young people to live lives guided by morality, compassion and social responsibility.

Tragedy struck at a comparatively young age. On 6 June 1993, Mr. Dhira Sen Singha passed away during gallbladder surgery. Though his life was cut short, his contributions to the legal profession, the Buddhist community and society remain a lasting inspiration. He is remembered with deep respect and gratitude for his integrity, leadership and dedication to justice, humanity and the welfare of others.
