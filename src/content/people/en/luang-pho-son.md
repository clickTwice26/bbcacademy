Luang Pho was the 5th abbot of Wat Phuttha Bucha (Bang Mot Subdistrict, Thung Khru District, Bangkok), of the Dhammayuttika Nikaya. He was a practising monk of exemplary conduct, worthy of respect and veneration, possessing great merit and spiritual power, and deeply revered by Buddhists and his disciples.

He led the project to build a Thai-style Great Pagoda (Maha Chedi) to enshrine the Buddha's relics. Named Phra Maha Chedi Phuttha Bucha Maha Lokanat Pacha Samakkhi Si Analayo, it also includes a Dhamma practice centre. It stands at Wat New Salavan Vihara (New Salban Vihara) in Cumilla, Bangladesh, and is the first and only Great Pagoda with Thai architecture in the country.
