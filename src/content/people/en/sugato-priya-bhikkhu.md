Venerable Sugato Priya Bhikkhu was born in the village of Dupchar, Laksam, Cumilla. He is an heir and disciple of the Most Venerable 10th Sangharaj Jyotipal Mahathero. During his many years of higher study, and as a resident monk at Wat Phutthabucha in Bangkok, Thailand, he built warm relationships with the Thai people.

Under the guidance of his preceptor, Phra Thep Mongkhon Yan (Luang Pho Son Analayo), Chief Abbot of Wat Phuttha Bucha, and by inspiring devout and generous donors, he played a pioneering role in establishing the magnificent World Peace Pagoda Analayo within the sacred grounds of New Salban Vihara, with the grant contributions of benevolent Thai people.

In 2010 he brought a group of benevolent Thai devotees, together with the pious Mrs. Chitra Petvongs, to Bangladesh. They were astonished by the ancient Buddhist culture and heritage of Kotbari-Mainamati and Cumilla, and decided to build a unique Thai temple in the holy grounds of New Salban Vihara, a project of the Bangladesh Buddhist Cultural Academy. Venerable Sugato Priya Bhikkhu managed the drawings and design, and raised funds at home and abroad.
