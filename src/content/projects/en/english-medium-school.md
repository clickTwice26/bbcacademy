In today's world the need for English-medium schools keeps growing, as people prepare themselves for global communication, higher education and professional life. We have taken up this project with these goals:

- **An international-standard curriculum.** Following internationally recognised curricula such as Cambridge or Edexcel, so that students receive a world-class education.
- **Fluency in English.** Studying in English from an early age helps students become fluent and skilled in written and spoken English.
- **A path to higher education.** An English-medium background, including O-Level and A-Level qualifications, opens the way to leading universities abroad.
- **Modern teaching.** Teaching that goes beyond rote learning, with a focus on practical knowledge, creativity and analytical skills.
- **Co-curricular activities and life skills.** Special emphasis on sports, debating, cultural activities and leadership, alongside academics.

The Academy is right next to Cumilla town, an area known for its leading educational institutions, including Cumilla University. Yet there is no English-medium school or college in the immediate vicinity. Recognising the needs of the time, we are planning an English-medium school on our premises, but we do not yet have enough funds to carry out the project.

We are seeking funds for this project. Should any individual or group of individuals wish to take it forward, alone or together with an organisation, the authorities will welcome the initiative and give their full cooperation.
