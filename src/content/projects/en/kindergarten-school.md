Early education matters enormously in a child's schooling. Kindergarten is a vital first step that bridges the gap between home and formal school, building early academic, social and emotional skills through play.

The Salban Vihara Orphanage is home to 120 children, and most of them came to us at an early age, away from their families. Some of them are novices. So we have started a kindergarten for them, and for the helpless and destitute children of the neighbourhood, to give them a good start in their education.
